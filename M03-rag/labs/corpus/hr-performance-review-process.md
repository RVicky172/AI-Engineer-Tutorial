# Performance review calibration guide for managers (Confidential)

Reviews run from 1 to 31 March. Calibration meetings per department are held in the first two weeks of April.

## Rating distribution guideline
| Rating | Guideline share |
|---|---|
| 5 Exceptional | up to 10% |
| 4 Exceeds | about 25% |
| 3 Meets | about 55% |
| 2 Partially meets | up to 10% |
| 1 Does not meet | as needed |

The distribution is a guideline, not a quota. Teams under 8 people are calibrated together with a neighbouring team.

## Calibration rules
- Managers bring evidence (delivered work, feedback, incidents handled) for every rating of 4, 5 or 2.
- A rating of 2 requires a performance improvement plan agreed with HR.
- Ratings are not shared with employees until calibration is complete.
- Salary increases are calculated from the rating and the position in the salary band (see the salary bands document).
