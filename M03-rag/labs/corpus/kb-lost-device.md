# Report a lost or stolen device

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone **as soon as you notice**, even if you think you might find it again.

1. Call the service desk on extension 4357 (from abroad: +91 20 5555 4357) and say "lost device". It is handled as a security incident.
2. IT locks the device remotely, signs you out of every session, and removes company data from phones. Laptops are encrypted with BitLocker, so the files cannot be read without your PIN.
3. If it was stolen, report it to the police and send the report number to the service desk; the insurer needs it.
4. You get a replacement laptop the same or next working day.

Finding the device later is fine: bring it to the IT desk to have it checked and unlocked.
