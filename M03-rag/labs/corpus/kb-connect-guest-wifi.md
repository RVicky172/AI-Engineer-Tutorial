# Connect visitors to guest Wi-Fi

Visitors use the `ACME-Guest` network. Never give visitors access to `ACME-Corp`, and never connect a visitor's laptop to a network socket.

1. Reception creates a guest pass at `https://guest.acme.example` with the visitor's name and email. Passes are valid for up to 5 days.
2. The visitor connects to `ACME-Guest` and enters the code from the pass on the portal page.
3. Guest Wi-Fi is internet-only and limited to 20 Mbit/s per device.

For events with more than 25 guests, ask the service desk at least 3 working days in advance for an event code.
