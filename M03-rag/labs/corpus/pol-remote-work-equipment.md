# Equipment for remote and hybrid work

Every employee gets a laptop, a headset from the approved list and a laptop bag. Employees working from home at least two days a week may also order:

- one external monitor (24 or 27 inch),
- a keyboard and mouse,
- a docking station.

Order through the service portal ("Remote work equipment"). Your manager approves; delivery takes about 10 working days.

Equipment stays ACME property and is returned when you leave. Repairs are done by IT; do not open the equipment yourself.

Home internet is not reimbursed, except for employees whose contract says they work fully remotely. Printers for home use are not provided.
