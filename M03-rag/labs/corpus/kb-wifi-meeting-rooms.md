# Wi-Fi is slow in meeting rooms

Large meeting rooms have one access point for up to 30 people. When everyone joins a video call over Wi-Fi, each person gets less bandwidth.

- Use the room's wired conference system for the call instead of every laptop joining separately.
- Turn off video when you are not speaking.
- Make sure you are on `ACME-Corp`, not `ACME-Guest`, which is limited to 20 Mbit/s per device.

If the Wi-Fi is not just slow but completely unavailable on a whole floor, report it to the service desk as an outage.
