# Acceptable use of IT systems

This policy applies to everyone who uses ACME devices, accounts or networks.

## Devices
- Company laptops are for work. Limited personal use (reading news, personal email in a browser) is allowed if it does not affect your work or security.
- Do not install software outside Company Portal or disable security tools (antivirus, disk encryption, device management).
- Lock your screen (Windows key + L) whenever you leave your laptop.
- Do not connect unknown USB storage devices. USB storage is read-only on company laptops; exceptions need security approval.

## Data
- Store work files in OneDrive, SharePoint or approved shares, never on personal cloud services or USB sticks.
- Follow the data classification policy: Confidential and Restricted data may only be shared with people who need it.
- Public AI tools: do not paste Confidential or Restricted data into AI tools that are not on the approved list. The approved list is on the security intranet page.

## Monitoring
ACME monitors the use of its systems for security purposes, as allowed by law. Monitoring is done by the security team under the security incident response playbook; managers cannot request access to an employee's mailbox without approval from HR and legal.

## Breaches
Breaking this policy can lead to disciplinary action. Report suspected breaches to the security team.
