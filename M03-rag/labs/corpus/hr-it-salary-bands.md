# IT salary bands 2026-27 (Restricted)

Annual fixed pay in INR lakh, Pune and Mumbai. Variable pay is on top: 10% of fixed pay up to band L4, 15% from L5.

| Band | Example roles | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | service desk analyst | 4.5 | 5.8 | 7.0 |
| L2 | senior service desk analyst, junior engineer | 7.0 | 9.0 | 11.0 |
| L3 | engineer (network, platform, DBA) | 11.0 | 15.0 | 19.0 |
| L4 | senior engineer, team lead | 18.0 | 24.0 | 30.0 |
| L5 | principal engineer, IT manager | 28.0 | 36.0 | 44.0 |
| L6 | head of IT operations, architect | 42.0 | 52.0 | 62.0 |

## Rules
- New hires are offered at most the band midpoint without HR director approval.
- Salaries above the band maximum are frozen until the band catches up.
- This document is Restricted: managers receive only the bands for their own team.
