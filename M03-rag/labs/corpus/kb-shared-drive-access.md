# Get access to a shared drive or Teams site

Access to file shares and Teams sites is granted through groups, never to individual people.

1. In the service portal, raise an "Access request" and pick the share or site from the list.
2. The request goes to the **data owner** of that share (not your manager, and not IT). The finance and HR shares are owned by the heads of those departments.
3. When the owner approves, you are added to the group. Access works after you sign out and back in, or within 1 hour.

Access to the finance and HR shares is reviewed every quarter; owners remove anyone who no longer needs it.

If you only need one file, ask a colleague to share a OneDrive link instead.
