# Reset your SSO password

Your SSO password unlocks email, Teams, the ERP and most company apps.

## If you know your current password
Go to `https://id.acme.example/profile`, choose **Change password**, and follow the password policy (at least 14 characters, not one of your last 10 passwords).

## If you forgot it or are locked out
1. Go to `https://id.acme.example/reset` from any browser, including your phone.
2. Enter your work email address.
3. Approve the request in the authenticator app, or enter the code sent to your registered mobile number.
4. Choose a new password.

Your account locks for 30 minutes after 10 failed attempts. The reset page unlocks it immediately.

## Still stuck?
Call the service desk on extension 4357 (HELP). The service desk will verify your identity with your manager. **IT will never ask for your password**, by phone, chat or email.

After a reset, update the password on your phone's mail app, or it keeps failing and locks you out again.
