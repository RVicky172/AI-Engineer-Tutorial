# Your mailbox is full

Standard mailboxes hold 50 GB. At 49 GB you get a warning; at 50 GB you can no longer send mail, and at 50.5 GB you stop receiving it.

## Free space quickly
1. Empty **Deleted Items** and **Junk Email**.
2. Search for large messages: in Outlook, search `size:>10MB`, then save the attachments to OneDrive and delete the emails.
3. Old shared files: share a OneDrive link next time instead of attaching the file.

## Online archive
Every mailbox has an online archive (100 GB). Mail older than 2 years moves there automatically. You can drag folders into **In-Place Archive** in Outlook yourself.

Mailbox size increases are not granted for individual users; the archive is the supported answer.
