# Report a phishing email

## You received a suspicious email but did not click
Use the **Report Phishing** button in Outlook (top ribbon, or the "..." menu on the phone). The email is sent to the security team and removed from your mailbox. That is all you need to do.

## You clicked a link, opened an attachment or entered your password
Act immediately; minutes matter.
1. **Disconnect** your device from the network: turn off Wi-Fi or unplug the cable. Do not shut it down; the security team may need its memory.
2. Call the service desk on extension 4357 and say "security incident". It is treated as a P1.
3. If you entered your password, the service desk resets it and revokes your sessions while you are on the call.
4. Do not delete the email; report it with the button so security can block it for everyone.

Signs of phishing: urgency ("your mailbox will be deleted today"), a sender address that does not match the name, links whose address does not match the text, and requests for passwords or MFA codes. IT never asks for your password or MFA code.
