# No sound or microphone in Teams calls

1. In the call, click **More (...) → Settings → Device settings** and check that the right speaker and microphone are selected. Teams often picks the monitor's speakers after you dock.
2. Make a test call: **Settings → Devices → Make a test call**.
3. Check the hardware mute switch on your headset, and the Windows sound settings (the microphone may be muted there).
4. Unplug and re-plug a USB headset; Bluetooth headsets sometimes connect in "hands-free" mode with poor sound: remove and pair them again.
5. Restart Teams completely (right-click the tray icon → Quit).

Only headsets from the approved list in Company Portal are supported for calls with customers.
