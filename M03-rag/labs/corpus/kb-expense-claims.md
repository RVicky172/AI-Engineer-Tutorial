# Submit an expense claim

1. Open the expense tool from `https://apps.acme.example` (it uses SSO).
2. Click **New claim**, choose the trip or "No trip", and add each receipt with the phone app or by uploading a photo.
3. Pick the cost centre; your manager approves the claim.

Approved claims are paid with the next salary run. The spending limits for travel, hotels and meals are in the travel and expense policy (PDF on the finance intranet). If the tool shows "invalid SAML response", it is an SSO problem; check the IT status page.
