# Clear a stuck print queue on your laptop

Your document sits in the queue with status "Error" or "Printing" and nothing comes out, while colleagues can print to the same printer.

1. Open **Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners**, select the printer, and choose **Open print queue**.
2. Cancel all documents. If they do not disappear, continue.
3. Open the Company Portal app and run the **Fix printing** tool. It restarts the Print Spooler service on your laptop and clears stuck jobs. You do not need admin rights.
4. Print again.

If nobody on your floor can print, the problem is the central print server, not your laptop: report it to the service desk.
