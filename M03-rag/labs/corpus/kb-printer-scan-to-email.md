# Scan to email from an office printer

1. Tap your badge on the printer's card reader.
2. Choose **Scan → Scan to Me**. The scan is emailed to your work address as a PDF.
3. For more than 20 pages, use the document feeder on top, face up.

If the printer shows an error code while scanning, see "Office printer error codes". Scans larger than 25 MB are split into several emails.
