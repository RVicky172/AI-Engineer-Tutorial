# First day: set up your laptop

Your laptop arrives pre-registered to you. Setup takes about 45 minutes; do it on the office network or at home with a reliable connection.

1. Start the laptop and connect to Wi-Fi (`ACME-Corp` in the office, your home network elsewhere).
2. Sign in with the work email and the temporary password from your welcome email.
3. Set a new password (at least 14 characters) when asked.
4. Set up MFA in Microsoft Authenticator when asked. Install it on your phone first.
5. Windows Autopilot installs the standard apps (Office, Teams, Company Portal, VPN client). Leave the laptop on until the progress screen says **Complete**.
6. Set a Windows Hello PIN (6 digits or more).
7. Open Outlook and Teams once so they finish configuring.

Problems on the first day are handled with priority: call the service desk on extension 4357 and say you are a new starter.
