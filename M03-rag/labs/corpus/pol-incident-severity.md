# Incident priority definitions

The service desk sets the priority when an incident is logged and changes it when the impact changes.

| Priority | Definition | Response target | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Critical | more than 20 people blocked; a business-critical system (email, ERP, production databases, web shop) down; or a suspected security compromise | 15 minutes, 24/7 | 4 hours |
| P2 High | a team of 2-20 people blocked; a business-critical system degraded; or one person blocked on a deadline today | 30 minutes, business hours | 8 business hours |
| P3 Medium | one person blocked or degraded with a workaround; non-critical system issue | 4 business hours | 3 business days |
| P4 Low | requests, questions, how-to and cosmetic issues | 1 business day | 10 business days |

Business hours are 08:00-20:00 IST, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in Maharashtra.

A P1 always starts the major incident process. A suspected compromise is P1 even if only one person is affected.
