# Incident priority definitions (2024, superseded)

> Superseded by the 2026 version. Kept for audit purposes.

| Priority | Definition | Response target | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Critical | a business-critical system down for many users | 30 minutes, 24/7 | 8 hours |
| P2 High | a department blocked or a critical system degraded | 1 hour, business hours | 2 business days |
| P3 Medium | one user affected | 1 business day | 5 business days |
| P4 Low | requests and questions | 2 business days | 15 business days |

Business hours were 09:00-18:00 IST, Monday to Friday.
