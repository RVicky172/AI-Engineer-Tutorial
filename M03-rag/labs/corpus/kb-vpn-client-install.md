# Install the VPN client

Company laptops get the VPN client automatically. To reinstall it:

1. Open **Company Portal** and search for "ACME VPN".
2. Click **Install**. It takes about 2 minutes.
3. Restart the laptop.
4. Open the client and connect to **Automatic**. Sign in with SSO when asked.

Personal computers cannot use the VPN. Use the web apps at `https://apps.acme.example` instead.
