# Install or request software

## Approved software
Open the **Company Portal** app on your laptop. Everything listed there is approved and installs without admin rights, for example Python, VS Code, Zoom, Adobe Reader and 7-Zip.

## Software that is not in Company Portal
1. Raise a "Software request" in the service portal with the product name, version, and why you need it.
2. The request goes to your manager for approval, then to the security team for a review. Reviews take up to 5 working days; paid licences also need a cost centre.
3. Once approved, the software is added to Company Portal for you.

Users do not get local admin rights. Browser extensions are limited to the approved list in the same portal.
