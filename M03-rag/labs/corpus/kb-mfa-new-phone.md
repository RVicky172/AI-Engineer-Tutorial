# Move MFA to a new phone

ACME uses the Microsoft Authenticator app for multi-factor authentication (MFA).

## You still have the old phone
1. Install Microsoft Authenticator on the new phone.
2. On a computer, go to `https://id.acme.example/security-info` and sign in (approve on the **old** phone).
3. Choose **Add sign-in method → Authenticator app** and scan the QR code with the new phone.
4. Approve a test notification on the new phone.
5. Delete the old phone from the list.

## You lost the old phone or it was wiped
You cannot add a new method without approving on the old one. Call the service desk on extension 4357. After verifying your identity with your manager, they issue a **Temporary Access Pass** valid for 8 hours, which you use in step 2 instead of the old phone.

If the lost phone also had company email on it, report it as a lost device too (see "Report a lost or stolen device").
